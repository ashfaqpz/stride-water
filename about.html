Who We Are

A Water & Wastewater Division Built on Engineering Discipline

Stride Waters is a division of Stride Haven Engineering Solutions FZE, established with the vision of extending our group's engineering, integration, and manufacturing expertise into the water and wastewater treatment sector.

Our team of engineers apply the same discipline used across oil & gas, power, and process industries to design, fabricate, and commission treatment systems that are reliable in the field — not just on paper. From reverse osmosis skids to full wastewater treatment plants, we manage the complete lifecycle: design, engineering, fabrication, installation supervision, and commissioning.
