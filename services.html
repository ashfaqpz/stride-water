Full-Scope Capability

Water & Wastewater Treatment Technologies

From raw water intake to final effluent discharge, Stride Waters engineers, fabricates, and commissions treatment systems tailored to your capacity, chemistry, and site conditions.

01

Reverse Osmosis (RO)

Custom-designed RO systems using semi-permeable membranes to remove dissolved solids, ions, and molecules — eliminating over 99% of contaminants. Available as skid-mounted or containerized units, with capacities from 10 up to 50,000 m³/day. Includes design, engineering, fabrication, installation supervision, and commissioning.

02

Ultrafiltration (UF)

Membrane-based filtration that removes suspended solids, bacteria, and pathogens ahead of RO or as a standalone polishing step — improving water quality and protecting downstream equipment.

03

Clariflocculation

Combined clarification and flocculation processes that remove suspended particles and turbidity, producing clear, pre-treated water ahead of further filtration or discharge.

04

Multimedia Filtration

Layered filter media systems that remove sediment, silt, and larger particulates as a robust, low-maintenance pre-treatment stage.

05

Domestic Wastewater Treatment

Compact, reliable treatment plants designed for residential communities, labor accommodations, and commercial developments — engineered for consistent effluent quality and easy operation.

06

Industrial Wastewater Treatment

Process-specific treatment trains including MBBR, MBR, and SBR technologies, engineered to handle demanding industrial effluent loads and meet discharge regulations.

07

MBBR / MBR / SBR Systems

Biological treatment technologies — Moving Bed Biofilm Reactor, Membrane Bioreactor, and Sequencing Batch Reactor — selected and sized to match your effluent characteristics and footprint constraints.

08

Sludge Dewatering

Mechanical dewatering solutions that reduce sludge volume for cost-effective handling, transport, and disposal.
